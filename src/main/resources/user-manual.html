1. Introduction

Sport Management System is a tactical manager game in the tradition of Football Manager and Championship Manager. You pick a sport, inherit a league of 16 randomly generated teams, and guide one of them through a full double round-robin season. Matches are simulated based on your players' attributes, the tactic you pick, and a little luck.

2. Installation

Run the provided SportManager-3.0.0.msi installer on Windows. The installer creates a Start menu shortcut and an optional desktop icon. No Java installation is bundled; the app expects a JRE 17+ already present on the machine, as specified by the course requirements.

If you are running from source instead of the installer, see README.md for the Maven commands.

3. Starting a new season

From the welcome screen:

  1. Pick Football or Volleyball from the dropdown.
  2. Click Start new season.
  3. The app generates 16 teams, each with a full roster, a head coach, and a starting tactic.

To continue a saved career, pick a slot in the Continue a saved game section and click Load.

4. The interface

The main window has a sidebar on the left and a content area on the right. The sidebar sections are:

SectionWhat it shows
OverviewSeason summary, top of the table, next fixtures.
League TableFull standings with all statistics.
FixturesEvery match of every week, color-coded by status.
My SquadInspect any team's roster, coach, and tactic.
Match DaySimulate the current week's fixtures.

5. Match day

Click Match Day from the sidebar. The upcoming fixtures are listed. Before pressing Simulate week, visit My Squad and set the tactic for each team you care about. Click Simulate week to advance. Results appear in the same list, and the league table and fixtures views update automatically.

Injuries and fatigue accumulate automatically. Injured players are listed in My Squad with the number of games they will miss.

6. Tactics & training

Four tactics are available for every team:

  • Defensive – low pressure, narrow width. Less fatigue, fewer goals for and against.
  • Balanced – the default. Neutral on every axis.
  • High press – high pressure, wide shape. More goals, more fatigue.
  • Counter-attack – low pressure, wide shape. Punishes opponents who overcommit.

Every team also has a coach with a specialty (for example finishing or spiking). During each match week the coach runs training sessions that improve the relevant attribute for available (non-injured) players.

7. Saving and loading

Use the sidebar's Save game button. Pick a slot from 1 to 5; the app stores your whole season to saves/slot_N.ser. To load a save, use the Load game button and pick an existing slot.

Saves are written as Java object serialization. Do not edit these files by hand.

8. Sport rules

Football

  • Roster 18 (11 starters + 7 bench), 3 substitutions per match.
  • Two 45-minute halves. Points: W = 3, D = 1, L = 0.
  • Tiebreakers: points → head-to-head → goal difference → goals for.

Volleyball

  • Roster 12 (6 starters + 6 bench), unlimited substitutions.
  • Best of 5 sets. Sets 1-4 played to 25 points, set 5 played to 15. Win a set by 2.
  • Points: 3-0 or 3-1 winner gets 3, 3-2 winner gets 2 and loser gets 1.
  • Tiebreakers: points → wins → set ratio → head-to-head.

9. Tips

  • Rotate your tactic based on fatigue. High press is devastating early but burns out your squad.
  • Use the Squad screen to spot players whose fatigue is over 70 and plan around them.
  • Save before each match week — if a season goes off the rails, you can revert.
